About Me

From Wine Business
to Digital Growth

My story starts in the vineyards. With a background in enology, wine business and brand development, I spent years understanding what makes premium products resonate — the storytelling, the sensory experience, the psychology behind a purchase decision.

Then I took everything I learned and applied it where the numbers are visible. Through Vigna 360°, a real D2C wine brand I founded, I built a live growth marketing laboratory — handling ecommerce architecture, content strategy, paid media experiments, customer journey design and KPI analysis all at once.

Today I operate at the intersection of strategic brand thinking and performance marketing. I think in funnels, track in dashboards, and build in systems. I'm actively exploring AI and automation to add velocity to every growth lever.
